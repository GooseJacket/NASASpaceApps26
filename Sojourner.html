Left Earth: December 4, 1996
Mission: To learn about Mars
Stopped: September 27, 1997, when it was no longer able to send messages back to Earth.
Where it is now: Parked on Mars
Fun Facts:
  • A landing robot called Pathfinder helped Sojourner get to and land on Mars.
  • Sojourner had an X-Ray and 3 cameras.
  • Sojourner learned that Mars dust is magnetic, and that there used to be liquid water on Mars.
